Associates and joint ventures: the equity method proforma

An associate is not consolidated line by line. The group shows one line in the SOFP (investment in associate) and one line in profit or loss (share of profit). Seen in May 2026 (Tulip, before control), Jul 2024 (Platinum, Prestige), Mar 2024 (Prime), Nov 2024 (Arcadia).

Real example: Arcadia, 25% (Lumina, Nov 2024)

Arcadia's profit after tax was 746,647. Arcadia depreciates a 2,000,000 asset at 20% reducing balance; Lumina's policy is straight line over 5 years. Find the share of profit.

Depreciation under group policy: 2,000,000 ÷ 5400,000
Depreciation in Arcadia's books: 2,000,000 × 80% × 20%320,000
Extra depreciation to deduct(80,000)
Adjusted profit: 746,647 − 80,000666,647
Share of profit of associate: 666,647 × 25%166,662

Entry: Dr Investment in associate 166,662, Cr Share of profit (the ICAB answer credited "other income"; a separate line is better under IAS 1).

Unrealised profit with an associateDownstream (group sells to associate): remove the group's % of the unrealised profit from the group's profit and from the investment. Upstream (associate sells to group): remove the group's % from the share of profit and from group inventory. Never remove 100%; and the associate's balances are not eliminated (it is an outsider).
Becoming an associate in stepsSignature held 15% (IFRS 9, fair value 18 per share) then bought 25% more: cost of the associate = fair value of the old 15% (28,440,000 × 15% × 18 = 76,788,000) + price of the new 25% (127,980,000) = 204,768,000. Then add the 40% share of profit for the part-year before control (908,510). When it later becomes a subsidiary, remeasure to fair value and record the gain.
Joint venture

A joint venture (IFRS 11) is also accounted for by the equity method, with the same proforma. A joint operation is different: the investor recognises its own share of each asset, liability, revenue and expense. The papers have not yet asked for a joint arrangement explicitly, so expect the definition and the choice between the two.

Disposal of a foreign subsidiaryOn sale, the cumulative translation reserve is recycled to profit or loss as part of the gain. In Mar 2023 the net assets disposed were translated at the historical rate 12 for acquisition-date net assets and shown with the closing rate 15.5 for the proceeds (140,000 RMB × 15.5 = 2,170,000).
Hyperinflationary economyIf the subsidiary's functional currency is hyperinflationary (IAS 29), restate first using the general price index, then translate everything at the closing rate. Jul 2022 tested the restatement: PPE of 55,200 restated by the price index = 55,200 × 1,800/1,200 = 82,800.

Last checks before you hand in

(1) Does the SOFP balance? (2) Is goodwill shown once, in the consolidated column? (3) Did you remove the investment account and the subsidiary's share capital? (4) Is NCI shown in equity and in the profit allocation? (5) Did you apply time-apportion where dates are mid-year? (6) Did you remove both sides of every intra-group item? (7) Does each line have a Ref to a working?
