The one-page layout for any accounting issue

The suggested answers answer every issue in the same five steps. Copy the headings. Each step earns separate marks, so write all five even when the issue looks small.

  1. Issue and what management did. One sentence naming the transaction and the current (wrong) treatment.
  2. Rule. The standard and paragraph idea in one or two lines (for example "IFRS 15: recognise revenue when the performance obligation is satisfied").
  3. Calculation. A small table. Show every input from the question and the formula.
  4. Journal. Dr and Cr with amounts, and the correcting entry versus the draft.
  5. Effect. Profit, assets, liabilities and equity; one line of disclosure if asked, and the audit risk if the question is for an auditor.

Rules to quoteTaxable temporary difference (carrying amount above tax base for an asset) → liability. IFRS 2 Share-based payment: proformas

A. Equity-settled options (Jul 2025, Mar 2023 patterns)

100 employees, 1,000 options each, fair value at grant date 20, vest after 3 years of service. Expected stayers: year 1: 90, year 2: 88, year 3 actual: 87.

Year 1: 90 × 1,000 × 20 × 1/3600,000
Year 2 cumulative: 88 × 1,000 × 20 × 2/3 = 1,173,333; expense573,333
Year 3 cumulative: 87 × 1,000 × 20 = 1,740,000; expense566,667

Journal each year: Dr Employee expense, Cr Equity (share option reserve). Fair value is fixed at the grant date and never remeasured. Only the number expected to vest changes. A market condition (share price target) is in the fair value; the expense is recognised even if it is not met. A non-market condition (profit) changes the number expected to vest.

B. Cash-settled SARs

100 employees, 500 rights each, vest over 3 years. Fair value of a right at each year end: 22, 25, 30. Expected stayers: 90, 88, 87.

Year 1 liability: 90 × 500 × 22 × 1/3 (= expense)330,000
Year 2 liability: 88 × 500 × 25 × 2/3 = 733,333; expense403,333
Year 3 liability: 87 × 500 × 30 = 1,305,000; expense571,667

Journal: Dr Employee expense, Cr Liability. Remeasure the fair value at each year end and at settlement; the change goes to profit or loss. When the cash is paid: Dr Liability, Cr Cash.

Frequent errors

(1) Using the year-end share price for equity-settled options. (2) Forgetting the cumulative approach and expensing the full amount in year 1. (3) Treating SARs as equity. (4) Ignoring leavers. (5) Not accelerating on cancellation. (6) Forgetting that the ethical angle (Mar 2023) may ask whether management manipulates the profit target.
