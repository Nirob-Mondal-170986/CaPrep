1

How to write each adjustment

  1. Facts in one line with the amount and date.
  2. What management did (the draft treatment) and why it is wrong.
  3. Rule in one line (standard and idea).
  4. Calculation (present value, percentage, fair value) shown clearly.
  5. Journal with amounts, then the effect on profit and on net assets.
  6. If no entry is needed (a possible but unlikely lawsuit), say so and say disclose as a contingent liability.
Source note

Rows with amounts (Luna, Apon loyalty and forward contract, Bijoy plan asset loss, Splash interest-free loan, Astha penalties) follow the suggested answers I read. Other rows (including the Ambient currency loan, Zenith calves and Neon government loan) are my own treatment based on the Exhibit 5 titles and the standard involved; check the exact facts in the question before copying a treatment.

After the schedule

Summarise total effect on profit before tax; recompute current and deferred tax; show adjusted net assets and key ratios; flag the ethical point if management missed items that raise profit or hid items that cut it.

2

Revenue issues (IFRS 15)

Issue (case)Treatment and journal
Instalment sale, only cash recognised (Luna)Revenue at the present value of the consideration. PV of 3 equal annual receipts at the CAPM rate (13%) = 4,722,305; the rest (1,277,695) is interest income over 3 years. Dr Receivables, Cr Revenue.
Year-end exchange offer (Luna)Trade-in: revenue = cash received + fair value (NRV) of the old unit; the extra discount is not revenue. Dr Cash, Dr Inventory (old units), Cr Revenue.
Customer loyalty points (Apon, Neon, Biponi)Points are a separate performance obligation. Defer revenue for unredeemed points (expected redemptions × value): Dr Revenue, Cr Contract liability (Apon: 33,859,650). Redeemed points need no further entry if revenue was already reduced.
Advance for training (Marvel)Cash received in advance is a contract liability; revenue over the training as performed (half done at year end = half). Dr Revenue, Cr Contract liability for the rest.
Consignment sales (Bijoy)Control has not passed to the consignee: do not recognise revenue until the consignee sells to the customer; reverse sales and receivable, reinstate inventory at cost.
Product returns (Biponi)Expected returns: refund liability (reduce revenue) and an asset for the right to recover goods (reduce cost of sales).
Delivery charge (Biponi)If the company controls the delivery, it is a performance obligation: revenue when the delivery is done; if an agent, net.
Membership fee (Biponi)Fee for a period: contract liability, revenue over the membership period (straight line).
Commission earned, VAT on commission (Biponi)Revenue is the commission only (agent); output VAT payable on the commission; check VAT accounting and penalty exposure.
Referral commission paid (Marvel)Incremental cost of obtaining a contract: capitalise and amortise if recoverable and the benefit is over a year; otherwise expense.
3

Provisions, penalties, lawsuits and tax compliance (IAS 37)

Issue (case)Treatment and journal
Customer lawsuit, claim from vendor refused (Luna)Provision for the compensation (probable, estimable). The claim on the vendor is a contingent asset: do not recognise. Dr Penalty expense, Cr Provision 2,500,000.
Mobile court penalty and forensic audit fee (Astha)Expense and accrual: Dr Fines 500,000, Dr Professional fees 350,000, Cr Provisions. Possible further lawsuits with probability less than 50%: no provision, disclose a contingent liability.
Underpaid import duty and penalty (Luna)Duty on unsold goods in inventory cost; on sold goods in cost of revenue; penalty to expense; Cr Duty payable (3,000,000).
VAT claim or audit by authority (Marvel, Splash, PaperCraft)If probable that the authority will win and the amount is estimable: provision (and interest or penalty); if only possible: disclose. Reconsider VAT recoverable on expenses.
Legal action by a regulator or council (Marvel, Apon food safety penalty)Penalty assessed: expense and liability; pending action with probable loss: provision; otherwise disclosure.
Environmental contamination (Bijoy)Present obligation to clean up (law or public commitment): provision at the best estimate (5,000,000); Dr expense (or asset if it relates to the asset's cost), Cr Provision.
Lawsuit by an ex-employee (Apon, Infinity)Provision if probable; otherwise contingent liability; legal advice and probability matter.
Distributor dispute (Neon)Same test: probable outflow and reliable estimate gives a provision; otherwise disclose.
4

Assets: damage, revaluation, impairment, inventory, intangibles

Issue (case)Treatment and journal
Fire or vandalism or mob damage (Bijoy, Astha, Apon, Regal)Derecognise the damaged part at its carrying amount (loss to profit or loss). Insurance claim is an asset only when recovery is virtually certain; otherwise contingent asset. Replacement cost is capitalised as a new asset. Damage to an outlet under construction: write off abnormal cost.
Revaluation gain or surplus (Bijoy, Astha, Infinity)Increase to OCI (revaluation surplus), not profit; transfer the excess depreciation on the revalued part from surplus to retained earnings; recognise deferred tax on the revaluation as appropriate.
Reversal of an impairment loss (Neon)Allowed for assets other than goodwill, but only up to the carrying amount that would have existed (after depreciation) if no impairment had been recognised; check the evidence of changed estimates.
Carrying value assessment (PaperCraft)Compare carrying amount with recoverable amount (higher of fair value less costs of disposal and value in use); impairment loss if lower.
Inventory NRV (Luna, Infinity)Lower of cost and NRV. Luna: Colorion TV cost 14,000, discounted price 11,000: write down 3,000 × 3,500 = 10,500,000 (Dr Cost of revenue, Cr Inventory).
Expired goods in inventory (Neon)Write off to cost of sales (NRV is nil), consider penalties and the safety and ethical issue.
Replacement of a significant plant component (Splash)Capitalise the new component; derecognise the carrying amount of the old component (loss); depreciate the new part.
Purchase of kiosks (Apon)Capitalise purchase cost and installation; depreciate over useful life; check lease or buy.
Website, app, R&D (Luna, Marvel, Splash)Research and planning-stage costs expensed (SIC 32 for websites); development costs capitalised only if the six criteria are met; a customer database built internally is not recognised. Dr Expense, Cr Intangible assets.
Brand ambassador contract (Splash)Expense over the contract period (prepayment for the unexpired part); not an asset of the brand.
Biological assets: calves born (Zenith)Fair value less costs to sell at initial recognition and at year end (IAS 41): 20 calves × 25,000 − 25,000 selling cost = 475,000 gain to profit or loss; changes in average cost per animal are irrelevant.
5

Employees, tax and receivables

Issue (case)Treatment and journal
Gratuity (Astha)Defined benefit obligation by actuarial valuation, not cash basis; deferred tax asset on the liability (tax deduction when paid) = liability × tax rate. Recognise from the earliest affected year (restate comparatives) if prior years are wrong.
Loss of a plan investment (Bijoy)The company's loss on the bank deposit: Dr Loss 865,000 (1,000,000 + interest 65,000 − recovery 200,000), Cr plan asset; a shortfall in the defined benefit plan must be funded.
Wages, incentives and overtime unpaid (Infinity, PaperCraft)Accrue earned wages and incentives (IAS 19 short-term benefits): Dr Wages expense, Cr Accrual.
Current and deferred tax (Astha and all)Recompute current tax on adjusted taxable profit; deferred tax on temporary differences (revaluation, gratuity, provisions, depreciation); a Public Interest Entity must meet stricter reporting rules.
Long outstanding receivables (Marvel, Infinity)Expected credit loss: provision matrix by age; write off if irrecoverable; discuss credit control.
6

Financial instruments, foreign currency and government support

Issue (case)Treatment and journal
Forward contract for inventory purchase (Apon)A derivative at fair value through profit or loss unless hedge accounting is designated. USD 200,000 bought forward at 120; year-end rate 122: unrealised gain 400,000. Dr Derivative asset, Cr Exchange gain.
Interest-free loan from a parent (Splash)Measure at fair value using the incremental borrowing rate (13%): PV of 3 instalments of 1,000,000 = 2,361,153; difference 638,847 is an equity contribution from the parent; interest expense each year at 13% on the amortised balance.
Government loan below market rate (Neon)Fair value of the loan at the market rate; the benefit is a government grant (IAS 20) recognised in profit or loss over the related period.
Foreign currency loan (Ambient)A monetary liability is retranslated at the closing rate: USD 100,000 from 88 to 107 = exchange loss 1,900,000 (IAS 21). Exchange loss may be capitalised only to the extent it is an adjustment to interest cost on a qualifying asset (IAS 23). The related plant (non-monetary, at cost) is not retranslated.
Foreign currency payables and receivables (PaperCraft)Retranslate at year end; difference to profit or loss.
Investment in shares (Infinity)Measure at fair value (market price at year end) through profit or loss (or OCI if elected); recognise dividend income; classify properly.
Unpaid supplier or supply agreement (PaperCraft)Check for a take-or-pay or onerous contract (provision) and for unrecorded purchases.
Common errors

(1) Booking a provision for a lawsuit that is only possible. (2) Recognising an insurance claim that is not virtually certain. (3) Putting a revaluation gain in profit. (4) Using cash basis for gratuity. (5) Forgetting the tax and net asset effect of each journal. (6) Missing the ethical angle (management leaving out liabilities before an IPO).
