Formula sheet

Symbols: ke = cost of equity, kd = cost of debt, g = growth, T = tax rate, D and E = market value of debt and equity, Rf = risk-free rate, Rm = market return, DF = discount factor.

1. Investment appraisal

Item Formula Remember
Discount factor 1 ÷ (1 + r)ⁿ Use 3 decimal places
Annuity factor [1 − (1 + r)⁻ⁿ] ÷ r Equal flows from year 1 to n
Perpetuity Cash flow ÷ r Growing: next year's flow ÷ (r − g)
Money and real rates (1 + money) = (1 + real) × (1 + inflation) Inflated flows need the money rate
IRR L + NPV(L) ÷ [NPV(L) − NPV(H)] × (H − L) NPV(H) is usually negative, so the bottom adds
Sensitivity NPV ÷ PV of the flows affected After tax. Smallest % = most critical
Profitability index NPV ÷ outlay in the rationed period Divisible projects, one period only
Equivalent annual cost PV of costs ÷ annuity factor For replacement cycles of different lengths
Lease v buy rate Borrowing rate × (1 − T) Net advantage = PV buy − PV lease
Capital allowance tax saving Allowance × T Balancing allowance = written down value − scrap
Purchasing power parity Future rate = spot × (1 + inflation A) ÷ (1 + inflation B) Rate quoted as currency A per unit of B

2. Cost of capital

Item Formula Remember
Cost of equity (dividends) ke = D0(1 + g) ÷ P0 + g P0 is ex-div
Growth g = retention rate × return on equity; or (latest ÷ earliest dividend)^(1/n) − 1 n = number of years of growth
CAPM ke = Rf + β(Rm − Rf) (Rm − Rf) = market risk premium
Preference shares Dividend ÷ ex-div price No tax relief
Irredeemable debt Interest × (1 − T) ÷ ex-interest price
Redeemable debt IRR of: price now, interest × (1 − T), redemption Convertible: higher of redemption and conversion value
Bank loan Interest rate × (1 − T) Use book value as the weight
WACC Σ (market value × cost) ÷ Σ market value Market values, not book values
Asset beta (degear) βa = βe ÷ [1 + D(1 − T) ÷ E] Proxy's gearing
Equity beta (regear) βe = βa × [1 + D(1 − T) ÷ E] Your own gearing
Portfolio beta Σ (weight × beta) Weights by market value
Beta from data Covariance ÷ market variance = ρ × σ(share) ÷ σ(market)
Alpha Expected return − required return Positive = under-valued
Two-share portfolio risk σp = √[w₁²σ₁² + w₂²σ₂² + 2w₁w₂ρσ₁σ₂]

3. Financing and dividends

Item Formula Remember
TERP (N × cum-rights price + issue price) ÷ (N + 1) 1-for-N issue
Value of a right TERP − issue price Per new share. ÷ N for per existing share
EPS (PBIT − interest) × (1 − T) ÷ shares Less preference dividend if any
Gearing Debt ÷ (debt + equity), or debt ÷ equity State which one you use
Interest cover PBIT ÷ interest
Share price (dividend growth) P0 = D0(1 + g) ÷ (ke − g) Gordon: g = retention × return
Residual dividend Earnings − equity share of the capital budget
Bond value Interest × annuity factor + redemption × DF At the investor's required return
Dividend cover and payout EPS ÷ DPS; DPS ÷ EPS

4. Working capital

Item Formula Remember
Inventory days Inventory ÷ cost of sales × 365 Raw materials ÷ purchases
Receivable days Receivables ÷ credit sales × 365
Payable days Payables ÷ credit purchases × 365
Cash conversion cycle Inventory days + receivable days − payable days
Cost of a cash discount [100 ÷ (100 − d)]^(365 ÷ days saved) − 1 Simple: d ÷ (100 − d) × 365 ÷ days
Finance cost of receivables Sales × days ÷ 365 × interest rate Factor: advance % at factor rate, rest at overdraft
Economic order quantity √(2 × order cost × annual demand ÷ holding cost per unit)
Current and quick ratio Current assets ÷ current liabilities; (current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities

5. Business valuation

Item Formula Remember
Net asset value Adjusted assets − liabilities Floor value
P/E value Maintainable earnings × P/E Cut the P/E 25–30% if unlisted
Earnings yield value Earnings ÷ earnings yield Earnings yield = 1 ÷ P/E
Dividend yield value Dividend ÷ dividend yield Minority holding
Free cash flow Operating profit × (1 − T) − extra non-current assets − extra working capital Add depreciation back only if it was deducted and not reinvested
Terminal value FCF × (1 + g) ÷ (r − g), or a multiple of the last FCF Discount with the last year's factor
Equity value PV of free cash flows + investments − debt
Maximum price for a target Target value alone + PV of synergy − integration cost
Buyer's gain Synergy − premium paid Premium = price − target value alone
Price after a share-for-share deal Combined value ÷ total shares after

6. Foreign exchange risk

Item Formula Remember
Bank quote rule The bank always wins Tk per foreign unit: pay at the higher rate, receive at the lower
Forward Foreign amount × forward rate Divide if the quote is foreign per Tk
Money market, payment Deposit foreign now → buy at spot → borrow Tk Deposit = amount ÷ (1 + foreign deposit rate × months/12)
Money market, receipt Borrow foreign now → sell at spot → deposit Tk Borrow = amount ÷ (1 + foreign borrowing rate × months/12)
Interest rate parity Forward = spot × (1 + rate A) ÷ (1 + rate B) Rate quoted as currency A per unit of B
Option: call or put Buy the contract currency = call. Sell it = put Exercise only if the strike beats spot
Option worst case Payment: strike + premium. Receipt: strike − premium
Futures contracts Amount ÷ contract size Buy the contract currency later = buy futures
Basis Spot − futures Falls evenly to zero at expiry
Futures gain or loss Price movement × contract size × contracts
Hedge efficiency Gain on futures ÷ loss on spot
Netting Convert all balances to the home currency and net off

7. Interest rate risk

Item Formula Remember
FRA Borrower buys (higher rate). Depositor sells (lower rate) "3–9" = starts month 3, ends month 9
FRA payment (Market rate − FRA rate) × principal × months ÷ 12 Receive if the market rate is higher
Interest rate futures Price = 100 − rate. Borrower sells. Depositor buys Contracts = loan ÷ size × months ÷ 3
Futures gain or loss Price movement % × contract size × 3/12 × contracts
Options on futures Borrower buys a put (cap). Depositor buys a call (floor) Options on FRAs: borrower = call, depositor = put
Collar Borrower: buy put, sell call. Depositor: buy call, sell put Cheaper, but limits the upside
Swap net payment (Floating received − fixed paid) × notional × months ÷ 12 Final cost = fixed rate + loan margin
Swap saving Difference in fixed rates − difference in floating rates − bank fee Split as the question says
