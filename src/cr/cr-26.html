1

From corporate responsibility to ESG

Corporate responsibility (being accountable to society beyond shareholders) has become ESG: environmental, social and governance factors that affect long-term value and are increasingly demanded by investors, lenders and regulators. ESG failures can be costly but not always fatal: BP's Deepwater Horizon spill (2010) brought huge fines and reputational damage, yet BP's brand had largely recovered by 2017.

Why companies reportInvestor and lender demand (green finance); regulation; reputation and licence to operate; risk management; staff and customer expectations; access to supply chains.
Risks in ESG reportingGreenwashing (claims not supported by facts); selective, unbalanced reporting; poor data systems; no common standards; limited or no assurance.

Bangladesh position

Bangladesh Bank's green banking guidelines (2011) led to standalone sustainability reports for banks from 2015. The Dhaka Stock Exchange issued a sustainability reporting guideline with GRI in 2020 to encourage listed companies. Bangladesh Bank SFD Circular 06 (26 Dec 2023) requires banks and finance companies to disclose sustainability and climate information based on IFRS S1 and S2, phased in from 2024. Check whether the FRC or BSEC has since made S1/S2 reporting wider or mandatory.

Interactive question pattern: BTSJ Ltd wants to start sustainability reporting

What should it consider?

Purpose and users; which framework (GRI for impacts, S1/S2 for investors, or both); materiality assessment with stakeholders; data systems and controls; targets that are measurable and credible; balanced reporting including bad news; cost; and whether to get assurance to avoid greenwashing claims.

Environmental and climate matters in the statutory audit

Climate risksPhysical: acute (floods, cyclones, fires) or chronic (rising sea levels, heat, water shortages). Transition: new laws, carbon pricing, technology change, shifting customer tastes, litigation. Highly relevant to coastal and low-lying Bangladesh.
ISAs affected (IAASB practice alert 2020)ISA 315: climate risks in the entity, industry, regulation and supply chain. ISA 320/450: qualitative materiality as users care more. ISA 250: environmental laws. ISA 540: estimates (impairment cash flows, useful lives, provisions). ISA 570: going concern in extreme cases. ISA 720: consistency of climate claims in the annual report with the accounts.
Financial statement effectProcedures
Impairment and shorter useful lives (stranded assets)Ask about planned asset changes due to environmental law or strategy; inspect impairment workings; check assumptions match climate commitments; minutes and trade press
Provisions and contingencies (clean-up, fines, restoration)Ask about policies to identify liabilities; environmental reports and inspections; legal letters; compliance with permits; check constructive obligations from public pledges (IAS 37)
Capital vs revenue spending on compliance; development costsTest classification against IAS 16 and IAS 38
Going concernBusiness model threatened by regulation or demand shifts
Disclosures and other informationConsistency between climate narrative and the numbers (e.g. net-zero targets vs asset lives)

Planning: understand the entity's environmental policy, legal requirements and history of breaches; consider using an expert (environmental engineer). Audit reviews: consider whether environmental matters cast doubt on going concern, and obtain written representations.

4

Social audits

A social audit evaluates an organisation's social impact and ethical behaviour against its stated aims and stakeholders' expectations: labour standards, health and safety, community impact, diversity, human rights in the supply chain, anti-bribery. Steps: set objectives and standards; consult stakeholders; collect evidence (interviews, site visits, records); report findings and recommendations; follow up.

Bangladesh link

After the Rana Plaza collapse (2013), buyers and the garment industry introduced factory safety inspections and remediation programmes. Social and compliance audits of RMG suppliers (fire, building and electrical safety, wages, working hours, freedom of association) are now central to supply chain due diligence and to exporters' access to global brands.

5

ESG assurance services

Assurance adds credibility to sustainability reports. Standards: ISAE 3000 (general), ISAE 3410 (greenhouse gas), and the new ISSA 5000 (sustainability assurance). Mostly limited assurance today, moving towards reasonable assurance in some regimes (EU).

AcceptanceIndependence (especially if also auditor: self-review if the firm helped prepare the report); competence, including experts (engineers, scientists); suitable criteria (S1/S2, GRI, the entity's own definitions?); measurable subject matter; data systems; scope (which KPIs, which sites); level of assurance; who will rely on it; liability.
Evidence on typical claimsWaste or emissions reductions: meter readings, invoices from waste contractors, calculations with recognised emission factors, site visits. Community spending: payments to bank and recipients' confirmations. Staff and safety data: HR and incident records. Targets: board minutes and plans; check they are realistic.

Services also include due diligence on ESG risks in acquisitions, reviewing green bond use of proceeds, and helping clients build ESG data controls (watch independence for audit clients).

6

Integrated reporting and performance information

Integrated reporting (<IR>) explains how an organisation creates value over the short, medium and long term using six capitals. The IIRC merged into the Value Reporting Foundation, which joined the IFRS Foundation in 2022 (alongside the ISSB); the <IR> Framework is now maintained by the IFRS Foundation.

Financial

Funds from debt, equity, grants, operations.

Manufactured

Buildings, equipment, infrastructure.

Intellectual

Patents, systems, know-how, brand.

Human

Skills, experience, motivation.

Natural

Water, land, air, minerals, biodiversity.

Social and relationship

Trust with communities, customers, suppliers.

Capitals interact (buying IT raises manufactured capital but uses financial capital); the aim is long-term value, not a money value for everything; materiality is about what affects value creation. Implications: better joined-up thinking and investor understanding, but cost, new data systems and judgement.

Auditing performance informationIs the KPI relevant, clearly defined and consistently measured? Are systems and controls over the data reliable? Test source data (counts, records); recalculate; compare with prior periods and peers; check the narrative is balanced.
Concluding and reportingUsually ISAE 3000 limited assurance on selected KPIs. Describe the criteria, procedures and inherent limits (estimates, emission factors). Report exceptions; consider ISA 720 if in the annual report.
Exam focus

Scenarios ask how ESG matters affect the financial statement audit (impairment, provisions, going concern, other information), whether to accept an ESG assurance engagement, what evidence supports specific claims, and whether draft disclosures meet IFRS S1/S2. Watch for greenwashing, inconsistent targets, and self-review threats.
