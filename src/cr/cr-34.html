1

The ratio sheet

Use the same groups as the official answers: liquidity, solvency (gearing), profitability, efficiency; add cash-flow and investor ratios when the data allow. Always show two years and the % change, and the formula.

Marks lost

A page of ratios with no words. In the official answers (for example TOL, Mar 2022) every group has a paragraph: what the ratio is, the change, the cause from the scenario, and the effect on the business. Also, do not mix bases between years.

2

The four-part comment: Observation, Cause, Result, Recommendation

PartWhat to write
ObservationThe number and the direction: "Current ratio fell from 1.75 to 1.47 (−16%)."
CauseThe reason from the scenario or other ratios: "because trade payables grew and cash was used to buy plant."
ResultWhat it means: "TOL can still meet short-term debts, but almost all current assets are needed, so there is a liquidity risk."
RecommendationOne action: "Invest more in working capital; negotiate longer credit; review inventory levels."
Model paragraph in the style of the official answers (TOL, Mar 2022)

Quick ratio: 0.66 against 0.99 last year.

The quick ratio fell by 33% to 0.66 (O). The fall is mainly due to a rise in current liabilities from customer advances while cash balances decreased (C). A quick ratio below 1 means TOL cannot pay all its current liabilities from cash and receivables alone and depends on selling inventory or on new borrowing (R). TOL should plan its cash flow, speed up collection of receivables and consider medium-term financing (R).

  • Use 2–3 lines per ratio and 1 paragraph per group. Finish with an overall conclusion: financial position (good, adequate, weak), trend (improving or worsening) and the single biggest risk.
  • Link ratios together: falling gross margin plus rising receivable days plus rising revenue is a classic sign of aggressive revenue recognition.
  • Compare with the industry or last year as the question provides; if neither is given, compare the two years.

4

Investment, bid and due-diligence report

Asked in Jul 2025 (Rose Fashion), Nov 2024 (Daily Fashion), Jul 2024 (Violette), Mar 2023 (AICL), Jul 2023 (Wonder Cement). The skeleton below earns most of the marks.

  1. Heading and purpose. To, from, date, subject; one line on what is asked.
  2. Adjustments. Table of errors found (Chapter 32–33) and the revised profit and balance sheet. Include hidden liabilities: bank loans inside payables (Wonder Cement), related-party payables, unrecorded provisions, unrecorded gratuity.
  3. Performance and position. Ratio sheet and four-part comments (Sections 1–2); growth rates; cash flow.
  4. Red flags and ethics. Misstatement, window dressing, going-concern doubts, related parties, insider information (Rose Fashion: do not trade on inside information).
  5. Recommendation. One clear choice: proceed, proceed at a lower price or with conditions (indemnities, warranties, earn-out, due diligence), or decline. Link to the objective (growth, diversification, liquidity). Compare two targets by EPS and risk when asked.
  6. Limitations. See the list below.
Limitations of ratio analysis (write 4 of these)

Based on historical data · depends on accounting policies (revaluation, depreciation) · can be distorted (window dressing, one-off items) · year-end snapshot misses seasonality · differences in size and industry · no industry average given · inflation · ignores non-financial factors (management quality, brand, contracts, legal cases) · uses unaudited data.

Skeletons: memo, report, briefing note, email

MemorandumTo / From / Date / Subject → Purpose (one sentence) → Issue 1 (five steps from Chapter 32) → Issue 2 … → Summary table → Next steps. Used for accounting treatments (Nov 2025, Mar 2022 Green Home, Nov 2023 quality control).
ReportTo / From / Subject → Executive summary (3 lines with the verdict) → Analysis (sections with headings and tables) → Findings and risks → Recommendations → Appendices. Used for performance reports (Galaxy, Mar 2024) and AI in audit (May 2026).
Briefing note to the audit teamPrepared by / Subject → Background in two lines → Risk areas with analysis support → Procedures (bullet list per risk) → Ethical points → Deadlines.
Email to the partnerSubject line naming the matter → "Dear [name]" → What I found → Threats and safeguards, or advice → Request or action required → Closing. Keep it short and professional (Nov 2022 ethical concerns email).
