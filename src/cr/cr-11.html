Basic EPS: earnings and weighted average shares

Basic EPS = profit or loss attributable to ordinary equity holders of the parent ÷ weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding
Earnings (numerator)Group profit after tax, less NCI share, less preference dividends: for cumulative preference shares the dividend for the period whether declared or not; for non-cumulative only if declared. Preference shares classed as liabilities (redeemable) are already in finance costs, so no further deduction. Also adjust for premiums or discounts on repurchasing preference shares and the accretion on increasing-rate preference shares.
Shares (denominator)Time-weight shares from the date the consideration is receivable (usually the issue date). Exclude treasury shares. Partly paid shares count as a fraction, in line with their dividend rights. Shares issued in a business combination count from the acquisition date. Contingently issuable shares count only once all conditions are met.

1 Jan: 2,000 issued, 300 held in treasury (1,700 outstanding). 31 May: 800 issued for cash. 1 Dec: 250 bought back into treasury.

Bonus issues, consolidations and rights issues

EventTreatment
Bonus issue, share splitNo new resources. Treat as if it happened at the start of the earliest period shown: restate comparatives. Applies even if the issue is after the year end but before approval
Share consolidationFewer shares, same resources: restate all periods. But a consolidation combined with a special dividend (in effect a buy-back at fair value) reduces shares only from the date the dividend is recognised
Issue at full market priceTime-weight from the issue date; no restatement
Rights issue below market priceContains a bonus element. Multiply shares before the issue by the adjustment factor = cum-rights fair value ÷ TERP, and restate prior-year EPS by the reciprocal

Diluted EPS: convertibles and ranking

Diluted EPS = (basic earnings + post-tax savings from conversion) ÷ (basic shares + potential ordinary shares), assuming conversion at the start of the year (or the issue date if later)

Rules: weight potential shares for the time outstanding; lapsed or converted ones count up to that date; use the conversion terms most favourable to the holder. Include only dilutive items (those that reduce EPS or increase loss per share from continuing operations). Add back the IAS 32 effective interest on the liability component, net of tax.

Worked example: convertible loan stock

24m shares; £8m 8% convertible (1 share per £2), liability component £7m at 10% effective rate; tax 20%; profit £15m.

Basic: 15 ÷ 24£0.63
Interest saved after tax: 7 × 10% × 80%£0.56m
Extra shares: 8 ÷ 24m
Diluted: 15.56 ÷ 28£0.56

Diluted EPS: options and contingently issuable shares

Options and warrants (treasury stock method): assume the proceeds buy shares at the average market price; only the shortfall ("free" shares) is dilutive. Dilutive only when the exercise price is below the average price. No earnings adjustment. For unvested share-based payment options, add the IFRS 2 expense still to be recognised (per option) to the exercise price.

Worked examples: vested and unvested options

Profit £30m; 60m shares (basic £0.50); 5m options at £21; average price £30. Unvested case: £15m of IFRS 2 expense still to come.

Vested: proceeds 5m × 21 = 105m ÷ 30 = 3.5m bought; free 1.5m30 ÷ 61.5 = £0.49
Unvested: price 21 + (15 ÷ 5) = 24; 120m ÷ 30 = 4m bought; free 1m30 ÷ 61 = £0.49

Presentation, restatement and audit

PresentationBasic and diluted EPS for profit from continuing operations and for total profit, for each class of ordinary shares, with equal prominence, on the face of profit or loss (even if negative). EPS for discontinued operations on the face or in the notes. Disclose the earnings and share figures used and reconcile them; list antidilutive instruments; describe share changes after the year end. Extra per-share measures must use the IAS 33 share number and be shown only in the notes.
Retrospective adjustmentRestate basic and diluted EPS of all periods shown for bonus issues, splits, consolidations and the bonus element of rights issues, including those after the year end. Also restate for prior period errors and policy changes (IAS 8). Do not restate diluted EPS for later changes in assumptions or conversions.

Audit points: EPS is a key investor ratio and drives share-based bonuses, so there is pressure to manage it. Agree earnings to audited profit, NCI and preference dividends; agree share movements to the share register, board minutes and Registrar returns; recompute weights, TERP and adjustment factors; check every potential share (convertibles, options, contingent consideration) is identified and correctly ranked; check the disclosures and restatements.

Bangladesh point

BSEC rules also require listed companies to report net asset value per share (NAVPS) and net operating cash flow per share (NOCFPS) alongside EPS, with explanations of significant changes. Check the current BSEC notification for the exact wording.

Common traps

Using the year-end share count instead of a weighted average · forgetting to restate the comparative for a bonus or rights issue · deducting the dividend on liability-classified preference shares twice · using nominal coupon instead of IAS 32 effective interest · including antidilutive items · forgetting the NCI share of profit in group EPS.
