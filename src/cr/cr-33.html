B. Borrowing costs (May 2026, KCP)

Capitalise only active period: 24 × 8/1216.0

Intangible assets (IAS 38)Research: expense. Development: capitalise only when all six are shown: technical feasibility, intention to complete, ability to use or sell, probable future benefits (a market), adequate resources, reliable measurement. Never capitalise internally generated brands, training or marketing (Mar 2022, Nov 2025). Website and software (Nov 2023): planning stage expense; application and infrastructure development capitalise; operating costs expense. Amortise from when available for use; test indefinite-life items yearly for impairment. Acquired customer contract: recognise at the PV of expected profit (King, Mar 2022: 763,636 over 2 years).
Inventory (IAS 2) and grants (IAS 20)Lower of cost and NRV. NRV = expected selling price − further costs to complete and sell. Machine cost 2.0 billion, sale 1.9 billion, modification 0.3 billion: NRV 1.6, write-down 0.4 billion (Nov 2024); a sale after year end confirming the price is an adjusting event. Slow-moving stock: allowance (Mar 2025: 750,000 × 85% = 637,500). Grant for an asset: deferred income or deduct from the asset, released over the life. Grant at nominal value (May 2026): fair value of the asset and grant both recognised (Option A) or both at the nominal amount (Option B).

IAS 33 Earnings per share

Basic EPS = (profit attributable to ordinary shareholders − preference dividends) ÷ weighted average ordinary shares Diluted EPS = (profit + after-tax interest saved on convertibles) ÷ (weighted average shares + potential ordinary shares)
Weighted average with a bonus issue (Nov 2024 GSC pattern)

20,000,000 shares at 1 January. On 1 July 4,000,000 new shares were issued at full market price. On 1 October a bonus issue of 1 share for every 8 held. Profit attributable 49,500,000.

1 Jan – 30 Jun: 20,000,000 × 6/1210,000,000
1 Jul – 31 Dec: 24,000,000 × 6/1212,000,000
Weighted average before bonus22,000,000
Bonus factor 9/8 applied to the whole year24,750,000
Basic EPS 49,500,000 ÷ 24,750,0002.00

A bonus issue brings no cash, so it is treated as if it had always been in issue and the prior-year EPS is restated. A rights issue has a bonus element: factor = fair value before the rights ÷ theoretical ex-rights price.

Diluted: 1,000,000 options at exercise price 30, average market price 40: free shares = 1,000,000 × (1 − 30/40) = 250,000. Diluted EPS = 49,500,000 ÷ 25,000,000 = 1.98. Convertible bond: add back interest × (1 − tax) and add the shares on conversion.

IAS 21 and IAS 29: functional currency and hyperinflation

Choosing and changing the functional currencyLook at the primary economic environment: currency of selling prices, of costs, and of financing; currency that mainly influences sales. Dynamic Apparels (Jul 2024) sold in several currencies: the tests decide. A change is applied prospectively from the date of change by translating all items at the spot rate on that date; no restatement of comparatives. Presentation currency may differ from functional currency.
Foreign-currency transactionsInitial recognition at the spot rate (or average). At the year end: monetary items at closing rate, difference in profit or loss; non-monetary at cost at historical rate; non-monetary at fair value at the rate on the fair value date. Contract value fixed in a foreign currency: translate at the date the contract was signed or delivered (Jul 2023 SBCL), and recognise exchange differences separately.
Hyperinflation (Jul 2022, Rose Trading)

Restate non-monetary items by the general price index; keep monetary items as they are.

Restated amount = historical amount × (index at end ÷ index at acquisition)
PPE: 55,200 × 1,800/1,20082,800
Difference 82,800 − 55,200 to the restated equity or profit or loss27,600

Show a gain or loss on net monetary position in profit or loss. Equity is restated by the index too. Then translate everything at the closing rate.

Update

IFRS 18 Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements (issued April 2024) replaces IAS 1 for years starting on or after 1 January 2027. It introduces new profit or loss categories (operating, investing, financing) and management-defined performance measures. Check the effective date and whether ICAB and the FRC have adopted it before using it in an answer.
