Scoreboard and patterns

Always askedAdjusted financial analysis with non-financial information · SWOT · NPV or critical evaluation of two options with a recommendation tied to mission and vision · financing alternatives (bank loan in all 13) · ethics · four-pillar sustainability · Executive Summary · assumptions stated, workings in annexures.
Financing options seenBank loan (13) · IPO with BSEC share premium (7: Luna, Bijoy, Astha, Apon, Infinity, Splash, PaperCraft) · LBO (1) · sale and leaseback (1) · venture capital (1) · sale of a business unit (1) · share exchange (1) · Qualified Investor Offer (2) · convertible debenture (1).
Recurring accounting issue typesRevenue (instalment sales, loyalty programmes, consignment, advances, returns, membership) · provisions and contingencies (lawsuits, penalties, VAT and duty claims, clean-up) · property damage, fire, vandalism and insurance · revaluation and impairment · employee benefits (gratuity, defined benefit plan, wages, incentives) · inventory (NRV, expired goods) · deferred and current tax · intangibles (website, app, R&D) · financial instruments (forward contract, interest-free and government loans, shares) · foreign currency · biological assets.
Recurring ethical themesOverstated or incomplete accounts before an IPO or loan · product safety and outsourced manufacturing · duty, VAT and tax compliance · weak or banned auditors and audit report quality · concentration of control and governance · worker conditions and unrest · environmental harm and clean-up · pricing and consumer fairness · pressure on advisers.
Pattern in the money questions

The board gives you the discount rate data (risk-free rate, market return, beta), the funds needed, the loan terms (interest, grace period, covenants, penalty), and the IPO plan (issue cost, premium, maximum % to sell). You must work out whether the plan survives the tests: NPV positive, loan serviceable, BSEC premium acceptable, ownership within the board limit.

Not yet seen, and what may come next

My judgement, not a forecast

The format is stable, so the main change is the industry and the twists in the issues and financing options.

Financing and investment twists not yet usedGreen bond or sukuk issue · rights issue · preference or convertible shares · lease finance (IFRS 16) as an alternative to a loan · government subsidy or SME refinancing · supplier credit · crowdfunding · mixed funding (part IPO, part loan) with WACC and gearing effects · replacing the NPV with IRR, payback and profitability index · NPV with tax and working capital together · joint venture accounting · foreign currency loan hedging · merger with share swap and goodwill.
Accounting issues not yet seen as a main issueSale and leaseback accounting · IFRS 16 lease modification · expected credit loss on trade receivables · hedge accounting · discontinued operation and held for sale (IFRS 5) · government grants (IAS 20) beyond a loan · segment reporting · related party transactions · events after the reporting period · prior year error correction (IAS 8) · cash flow statement preparation · sustainability disclosures (IFRS S1 and S2).
Sectors not yet usedPharmaceuticals, readymade garments and textiles, banking and NBFI, telecom, insurance, education, real estate and construction, logistics, power distribution, fisheries, agro-processing and food (beyond restaurants), shipping, fintech. Be ready with the ethics and sustainability themes of each (Chapter 9).
